## 1. Executive Directive
FeraSetu's headlines, headings, and interface microcopy must communicate value with absolute clarity, brevity, and factual honesty.

> *"Do not make users read more to understand what could have been communicated clearly in fewer words. We do not write headlines to sound clever. We write headlines to make value unmistakable."*

---

## 2. The Four Jobs of a Strong Headline
1. **Relevance:** Immediately relates to the user's immediate context.
2. **Clarity:** Plain, active human language without technical buzzwords.
3. **Value:** Unmistakable tangible benefit (e.g. 0% commission, direct customer ownership).
4. **Direction:** Aligns directly with the primary call to action.

---

## 3. Mandatory Pre-Push Verification
- Check all public and dashboard headings for factual accuracy.
- Verify semantic heading hierarchy (`h1` through `h6`).
- Verify empty-state guidance explains what is empty, why, and provides an actionable next step.
- Ban synthetic AI clichés ("unlock possibilities", "supercharge").
- Record audit results in `ui-5-test.md` execution history.
