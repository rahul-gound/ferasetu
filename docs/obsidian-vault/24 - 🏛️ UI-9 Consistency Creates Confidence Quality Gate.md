---

## 1. Executive Directive
FeraSetu must feel like one cohesive, expertly crafted, and professionally engineered product.

> *"Learn once. Recognise everywhere. Consistency creates familiarity and confidence without destroying creative merchant autonomy."*

---

## 2. Core Coherence Invariants
1. **Design Foundations:** Unified typography, 3D tactile button standards (`DESIGN.md`), and high-contrast Slate color palettes.
2. **Predictable Navigation:** Consistent sidebar order, persistent active route wayfinding, and standardized iconography (`lucide-react`).
3. **Harmonized Terminology:** Zero confusing synonym drift across views.
4. **WCAG 2.2 Alignment:** SC 3.2.3 (Consistent Navigation) and SC 3.2.4 (Consistent Identification).
