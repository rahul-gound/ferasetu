## 1. Executive Directive
FeraSetu's interface must create a meaningful, respectful, positive, and empowering emotional experience for independent business owners.

> *"We want users to feel understood, empowered, confident, and proud of what they build with FeraSetu. Emotional quality must exist in the actual experience, not just visual decoration."*

---

## 2. The Three Levels of Emotional UX
1. **Visceral (Appearance & First Impressions):** Tactile 3D buttons, crisp typography, generous whitespace, and balanced Sovereign Blue/Saffron Orange palette.
2. **Behavioral (Ease of Use & Mastery):** Seamless task completion (e.g. unified 1-page Add Product flow without tab jumping), instant visual feedback in <150ms, and clear error recovery.
3. **Reflective (Identity & Pride of Ownership):** Reinforcing merchant independence ("Your business is yours. The internet should be too.") and 0% platform commission.

---

## 3. Ethical Non-Negotiables
- Zero fake urgency, fake reviews, or manipulative countdowns.
- Zero guilt-based microcopy or shaming dialogs.
- Total transparency regarding fees, billing, and data ownership.
